# ACE Credit End State

ACE Credit's end state is not a single app. It is a governed financial empowerment operating system that supports nonprofit programs, commercial services, staff operations, participant journeys, grant-funded delivery, and measurable economic outcomes without collapsing the legal and operational boundaries between entities.

## Mature system vision

The platform should eventually allow authorized staff to manage a participant or client from intake through education, financial stabilization, credit-related support, workforce advancement, entrepreneurship where appropriate, and long-term outcome tracking.

The same core platform may support separate nonprofit and for-profit experiences, but entity, money, contracts, permissions, reporting, and data use must remain explicit and auditable.

## Participant experience

A participant should eventually be able to:

- complete secure onboarding and consent;
- understand program eligibility and next steps;
- access financial education and assessments;
- receive an individualized financial action plan;
- track goals, tasks, referrals, and progress;
- participate in approved credit recovery/building workflows;
- access workforce or income-advancement pathways;
- enter business-development pathways when appropriate;
- upload/request documents securely;
- communicate with staff;
- understand what data is being used and why;
- see progress toward meaningful financial outcomes.

## Staff operating system

Authorized staff should eventually be able to:

- manage caseloads, programs, cohorts, and services;
- see role-appropriate participant context;
- assign tasks and interventions;
- manage education/coaching workflows;
- track referrals and partner activity;
- manage approved credit-related workflows;
- record measurable outcomes;
- generate grant/program reporting;
- manage deadlines and obligations;
- use AI assistance for research, preparation, summarization, routing, and repetitive administrative work;
- review and approve consequential agent actions before execution.

## Grant and organizational intelligence

The mature system should preserve institutional knowledge instead of leaving it scattered across chats, inboxes, and spreadsheets. It should support structured records for:

- funders and opportunities;
- eligibility and deadlines;
- award history;
- allowable personnel/program costs;
- previous recipients and funded models;
- proposals and attachments;
- reporting obligations;
- outcome metrics;
- staffing/capacity plans;
- partnerships and contracts;
- decisions and research provenance.

## Agentic operations

Agents may eventually serve as operational workers for bounded tasks such as:

- researching grants and regulations;
- preparing draft application material;
- organizing evidence and source records;
- generating internal task plans;
- triaging operational queues;
- preparing reports;
- checking completeness;
- assisting coding and testing;
- running scheduled non-consequential workflows.

Agents must operate through explicit permissions, audit logs, approval gates, and revocable credentials. No agent runtime becomes the permanent brain or constitutional source of truth.

## Technical qualities

The mature platform should be:

- secure and privacy-conscious;
- auditable;
- modular;
- accessible;
- maintainable by coding agents and humans;
- resilient to runtime/provider changes;
- capable of supporting separate entities and programs;
- measurable at the participant, program, grant, and organization levels;
- API/integration friendly;
- designed for gradual automation rather than unsafe autonomy.

## Business and mission outcome

Success means ACE Credit can sustainably employ people to deliver high-quality services, prove outcomes with credible data, win and administer aligned funding, serve paying commercial clients separately where appropriate, and scale its reach without losing mission integrity or operational control.

The technology succeeds only if it strengthens the organization's ability to help Black women achieve greater financial stability, stronger credit, income growth, asset ownership, business success where appropriate, wealth creation, and economic independence.

## End-state test

The project is approaching its intended end state when:

1. one source of truth connects strategy, programs, participants, operations, funding, impact, and technology;
2. nonprofit and for-profit boundaries are enforceable in the system, not merely documented;
3. staff can operate core programs end-to-end without fragmented manual systems;
4. participants can see and act on their own pathway;
5. outcomes can be measured and reported credibly;
6. agents reduce administrative burden without gaining uncontrolled authority;
7. the organization can change AI runtimes without losing its rules, data, workflows, or institutional memory;
8. the platform increases the number and quality of people the organization can serve sustainably.
