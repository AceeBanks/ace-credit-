# G0-B7-C7 — Grant Draft Quality Rubric

**Document ID:** GS-G0-B7-C7-RUBRIC
**Status:** RATIFIED (Book 7 chapter C7)
**Engine:** `prototype/g0/evaluation/assertions.py` (deterministic gates)
+ rubric dimensions (subjective, after gates)

Multi-dimensional grant rubric — "sounds professional" is not quality.

## Dimensions

1. requirement coverage (deterministic, HARD)
2. funder/program alignment (rubric)
3. organization-specific grounding (rubric)
4. problem/need clarity (rubric)
5. proposed approach coherence (rubric)
6. outcomes/measures quality (rubric)
7. implementation realism (rubric)
8. budget/narrative consistency (deterministic where machine-checkable, HARD)
9. evidence/citation support (deterministic, HARD)
10. factuality (deterministic claim ledger, HARD)
11. internal consistency (deterministic)
12. specificity vs generic filler (rubric)
13. uncertainty honesty (deterministic + rubric)
14. readability/structure (rubric)
15. forbidden fabrication absence (deterministic, HARD)

## Deterministic checks first

- required sections present
- character/word limits
- dates/amounts consistent (deadline, ceiling)
- citations resolve
- claims map to ledger
- budget totals reconcile
- prohibited unsupported claims absent
- opportunity revision exact

Subjective rubric grading comes only after deterministic gates pass
(EVAL-LAW-004).

## Human edit burden

Track meaningful edits required after draft generation. Do not equate
stylistic preference with factual failure.
